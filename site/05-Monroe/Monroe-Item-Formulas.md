## Decisions that change the estimate most

- Foundation: 50 linked U saddles are used as pile-position proxies. An independent area-density benchmark is 18. Confirm the pile and bracket schedule before using either for procurement; bedframe hardware follows the catalog pile-based rule.
- Floor connections: 236 model plates are selected. The catalog currently rounds their base kit to a minimum of 250, while its rule note says 100 versus 250 needs confirmation. The linked floor clips also contain 3,858 typed fastener positions whose screw type and site/factory scope are unspecified; they are not silently added to PAN-006.
- Roof: 2,815.187565 SQFT of typed sheathing is the panel-area proxy. Truss screws use 50 linked truss labels x 8 assumed bearing screws, separate from roof-panel screws. Truss-stage board screws assume 10% site board coverage of roof area and ten screws per covered SQFT. Confirm the roof-panel assembly, purlin layout, slopes and fastening schedules.
- LED profiles: the raw family sum is 1,715.846715 FT and the spatially reconciled estimate is 1,251.137745 FT. Confirm profile types and any remaining separate parallel runs.
- HVAC: 9 wall-mounted all-in-one units are modeled. Branch box, refrigerant lines and outdoor-unit power are conditional zero in this scenario; their rows state the split-system fallback.
- Electrical: 149 modeled outlet-cover equivalents include quad and stacked boxes. The six high-amperage positions split into three 30-AMP and three 50-AMP outlets. The breaker count groups modeled outlets, lighting/fans, AC, water heater, battery and solar as a planning circuit proxy; no circuit schedule is modeled. Cable and fitting lengths are budget routes, and the 50-AMP connector product is undefined.
- Openings and plumbing: 20 operable and 4 fixed windows are modeled; only operable windows drive screen/handle allowances. 5 toilet rough-in cutouts versus 4 toilet fixtures require location reconciliation.
- Gutter and fire protection: 237.010389 FT of gutter is modeled; the 400.643339 FT exterior-wall run is only a fascia/soffit and fastener proxy. No sprinkler-head instances are modeled, so sprinkler quantities are planning allowances pending a fire-protection design.
- Assemblies and tools: the fire riser and fire-riser assembly may be one purchase; sand/leveling and self-leveling compound may be alternate methods. Reusable tools need an inventory check.

## Quantity conventions

- **Model quantity:** selected model elements or typed dimensions. Derived model allowances still require scope review.
- **Confirmed standard:** a saved user-approved fixed project quantity.
- **Catalog fixed allowance:** an existing fixed catalog number that still needs project approval.
- **Planning estimate:** an editable rate applied to a model driver or explicit project assumption.
- **Conditional zero:** a defined item is held at zero in the current model scenario; its alternate-system allowance is stated in the row.
- **Planning placeholder:** zero only because the catalog item is undefined; it is not a measured zero.
- Spare = round up(quantity × catalog spare percent, quantity increment). Base kit = round up(max(quantity + spare, minimum kit), quantity increment). Do not add pack rounding until item demand, inventory, extra quantities and product pack sizes are confirmed.

Edit `config/planning-allowances.json` or `config/monroe-planning-overrides.json`, then rerun `python scripts/build-monroe-estimates.py` to change planning rates.
